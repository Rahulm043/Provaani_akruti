### Key Technical Parameters & Why They Are Configured:
1. **LLM Temperature (`0.35`)**:
   * `0.1` was causing rigid greedy search and verbatim repetition loops when callers asked follow-up questions.
   * `0.35` gives the model natural flexibility to dynamically rephrase answers while staying strictly grounded.
2. **TTS Speed Multiplier (`0.9`)**:
   * Smallest AI `meher` voice speaks naturally fast over 8kHz telephony. `0.9` ensures crystal-clear Indian English, Hindi, and Bengali phone cadence.
3. **TTS Natural Breath Pauses**:
   * Smallest AI TTS ignores `-` and `—`. We use commas `,` (150–200ms pause) and ellipsis `...` (300–400ms pause) to enforce realistic breathing cadence.
4. **Acoustic STT Keyword Biasing**:
   * `service_factory.py` passes procedure keywords into `wss://api.smallest.ai/waves/v1/stt/live?keywords=...`.

## 4. Key Learnings & Solved Edge Cases

1. **The *"Black board"* / *"ব্লকরো"* STT Mishearing**:
   * *Problem*: Telephone compression + North Indic acoustic models misheard *"Blepharoplasty"* as *"Black board"* or *"ব্লকরো প্লাস্টিক"*.
   * *Solution*: The **Phonetic Mishearing & Layman Aliasing Table** in the prompt maps these mishearings seamlessly to the right procedure.
2. **The *"Dar"* Pronunciation Bug**:
   * *Problem*: Smallest AI Bengali TTS synthesized abbreviated `ডা.` as *"Dar"* or *"Daa"*.
   * *Solution*: The prompt strictly enforces writing out the full word **`ডাক্তার`**, **`डॉक्टर`**, and **`Doctor`**.
3. **Mid-Call Language Jumps**:
   * *Problem*: In Run 47, an STT error transcribed a Bengali word into Devanagari (`बोलते`), causing the LLM to switch into Hindi.
   * *Solution*: Permanent Sticky Language Lock. The model stays in the chosen language regardless of STT script noise unless an explicit command like *"Please speak in English"* is uttered.
4. **Unprompted Fact-Dumping**:
   * *Problem*: The model recited clinic hours, two phone numbers, and email IDs on basic questions.
   * *Solution*: Hard 2-sentence cap + strict rule forbidding volunteering contact info unless explicitly asked.
